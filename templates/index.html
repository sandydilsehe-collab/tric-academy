About Our Academy

Nurturing Swimmers with Discipline & Safety

Established in Velampalayam, Tirupur, TRIC Academy operates with rigorous adherence to water safety, personal hygiene, and modern stroke mechanics under qualified coaches.

Learn to Swim Program

Beginner-friendly curriculum covering water comfort, buoyancy, breathing techniques, and fundamental strokes (Freestyle, Backstroke) in safe, supervised shallow-to-deep lanes.

Elite Competition Batch

High-performance squad training for district, state, and national meets. Dedicated race timing tracking, stroke analysis, split pacing, and endurance conditioning.

Strict Hygiene & 32 Rules

Daily water filtration, compulsory shower & swim caps, medical screening, and 32 enforced pool regulations ensure a clean and protective environment for every swimmer.
